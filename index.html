The Show

PORCELAIN CATWALK is Julian Vogel's new solo show. It fuses circus, music and fashion into a catwalk like no other. A vulnerable, poetic performer wears clothes made of porcelain, second skin and armour at once, and throws knives made of ceramic. Every move makes a sound.
